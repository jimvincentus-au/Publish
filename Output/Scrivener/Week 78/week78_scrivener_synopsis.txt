Week 78 measured almost no net movement, but stillness masked intense pressure on both sides. Executive power expanded through resumed Iran strikes, voter-roll purges, press subpoenas, and the firing of a judicially appointed prosecutor within an hour of his swearing-in. Three non-target civilians died in ICE operations within days; a brief safety pause was personally overturned by the president. Trump used a primetime address built on selectively declassified intelligence to relitigate the 2020 election while threatening broadcast licenses, even as CNN, a Republican congressman, and a sitting senator contradicted his claims within hours. Courts delivered mixed results: one judge voided a self-dealing IRS settlement and sanctioned Trump's lawyers, while another dismissed Proud Boys convictions at DOJ's request. Communities organized, a senator was pepper-sprayed, and the moral floor held, barely, through resistance outside the executive's control.
