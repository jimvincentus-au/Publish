Week 22 saw no single rupture, but a flood of coercive activity that outpaced institutional response. The administration directed ICE to target Democratic-run cities by name, deployed troops to Los Angeles, and reversed a brief pause on workplace raids. An Oregon vineyard manager was detained, protected-area guidance for schools and churches was withdrawn, and journalist Mario Guevara was arrested and transferred to ICE custody. Nationwide No Kings protests drew massive crowds, met by tear gas, prosecutions, and a Florida official's suggestion that drivers could run over demonstrators. Courts blocked some actions, including unlawful NIH grant terminations and passport restrictions, but appellate rulings also preserved federal troop control pending appeal. The EPA dropped an enforcement case against a major Trump donor, crypto oversight loosened, and Voice of America lost 639 employees. The public clock barely moved, concealing a widening gap between accountability and executive speed.
