This week's dominant story was the fallout from the killing of Alex Pretti, a VA nurse shot by federal immigration agents during the Trump administration's 'Operation Metro Surge' in Minneapolis. The shooting, and a second death of bystander Renee Good, ignited nationwide protests, a bipartisan push for congressional hearings and impeachment of DHS Secretary Kristi Noem, and a direct standoff between federal courts and ICE leadership over compliance with dozens of court orders. Separately, immigration enforcement expanded into schools, churches, and homes, including the detention of a five-year-old boy and other children. The week also saw a partial-shutdown fight in the Senate over DHS
