Title
-----
The Week Power Moved Quietly

Subtitle
--------
A near-motionless clock concealed eighteen structural shifts toward erosion, driven by delayed disclosure, expanded detention, and legal process turned political.

Tagline / Hook
-------------
Some weeks announce their damage. This one accumulated it quietly, through redaction, delay, and infrastructure that will outlast the news cycle.

Synopses
--------
Long Synopsis:
In the week ending December 26, 2025, the Democracy Clock moved by only one-tenth of a minute, but that stillness masked eighteen distinct trait shifts, almost all toward erosion. The Justice Department missed a legal deadline on Epstein files, released heavily redacted records, disappeared and restored documents, then claimed to have found over a million more pages. Immigration enforcement expanded through a $170 billion buildout, record detention, and plans for 80,000-person facilities. Courts pushed back repeatedly, blocking National Guard deployment and coercive funding cuts. Congress showed split instincts, forcing votes while leadership limited scrutiny. Press freedom faced new pressure as Trump called the New York Times a national security threat and CBS pulled a vetted segment. The week's danger lay in its quietness.

Short Synopsis:
The clock barely moved, but eighteen traits shifted toward erosion as the Justice Department stalled Epstein transparency, immigration enforcement expanded through detention and force, and courts provided the week's most consistent check on executive reach.
