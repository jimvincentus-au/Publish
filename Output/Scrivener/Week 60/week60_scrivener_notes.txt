Synopses
--------
Long Synopsis:
Week 60 opened with President Trump launching unauthorized strikes on Iran, then escalating rhetoric toward regime change and assassination while denying responsibility for a strike that killed roughly 175 people at a girls' school. He threatened to veto all legislation unless Congress passed the Save America Act, a sweeping voting-restriction bill, and pressed the Senate to eliminate the filibuster to force it through. Majority Leader Thune refused. Courts pushed back elsewhere too: voiding Kari Lake's unlawful Voice of America leadership, invalidating unconfirmed prosecutors, blocking warrantless ICE arrests, and quashing a retaliatory subpoena against Fed Chair Powell. Meanwhile, immigration enforcement targeted Somali American communities as oversight offices were gutted, and press freedom came under direct regulatory threat. The clock moved only four-tenths of a minute, but the forces beneath it did not sit still.

Short Synopsis:
An unauthorized war, a coerced voting-restriction bill, and denied civilian deaths met resistance from courts, a Senate leader, and reporters this week. The clock barely moved, a net loss of twenty-four seconds, masking two roughly equal forces grinding against each other inside the machinery of government.
