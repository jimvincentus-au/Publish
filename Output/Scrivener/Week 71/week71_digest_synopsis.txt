The week was defined by a widening use of state power around immigration, information control, and public money. The administration rolled out a major change to green-card processing that would force many applicants to leave the country, while federal officials escalated pressure on sanctuary cities and protesters outside a New Jersey detention center. At the same time, courts became a central counterweight: judges blocked Alabama's congressional map, halted Trump's proposed anti-weaponization fund, and ruled that the Kennedy Center could not be renamed for him without Congress.
