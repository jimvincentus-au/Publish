This week the conflict between federal enforcement power and local and constitutional limits moved from courtrooms into the streets. A record-breaking immigration crackdown in Los Angeles, including workplace raids, military-style tactics, and a demand for 3,000 daily arrests, triggered mass protests, a National Guard federalization fight with California, and a widely seen incident in which a sitting U.S. senator was forced to the ground and handcuffed while trying to ask a question. Courts pushed back repeatedly, blocking parts of the deportation and election agendas, though appellate stays kept some federal actions in place.
