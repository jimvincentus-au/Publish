This week compressed an unusual number of institutional stress points into seven days. Trump ordered troops into Portland and, in a rambling address to military leadership at Quantico, described American cities as war zones and floated using them as military training grounds. Simultaneously, the Justice Department indicted former FBI Director James Comey and New York Attorney General Letitia James, both perceived political opponents, after removing prosecutors who resisted the charges. A government shutdown began Wednesday after funding talks collapsed over healthcare subsidies, and the administration immediately used the lapse to freeze billions in infrastructure and climate funding
