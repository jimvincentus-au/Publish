This week's defining story was Minnesota. Following the fatal shooting of Renee Good by an ICE agent, the administration flooded the Twin Cities with roughly 3,000 federal agents, opened criminal investigations into Governor Walz and Mayor Frey, and had the Justice Department decline to investigate the shooting itself—triggering prosecutor resignations. ICE agents detained a five-year-old boy as bait for his father, forcibly held a U.S. citizen without a warrant, and operated under a leaked memo claiming authority to enter homes without judicial warrants. Courts pushed back—federal judges barred pepper-spray use on protesters—only for an appeals court to lift that protection days later.
