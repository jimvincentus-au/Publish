Title

A Standing Clock Amid Widening War Powers

Subtitle
--------
An unauthorized war expanded and coercive enforcement deepened at home, yet the measured week only confirmed risks already scored, not new ones.

Tagline / Hook

Synopses
--------
Long Synopsis:
The Democracy Clock held at 7:43 p.m. this week, unchanged from the prior period, even as Trump ordered unauthorized combat operations against Iran and Congress failed twice, in the House and Senate, to reclaim war powers. Pentagon officials had told staff Iran showed no clear intent to strike first, a detail that did not slow the campaign. Domestically, the House passed the SAVE America Act tightening voter documentation rules, the FBI searched Fulton County's election office, and DHS pursued naturalized citizens over past voting. Kristi Noem was fired amid a bruising hearing and inspector-general obstruction findings, then replaced by a loyalist senator. DOJ directed lawyers to serve Trump's interests personally. Florida advanced bills treating dissent as a security threat. Courts, including rulings on tariffs, detention oversight, and congressional access, provided the week's clearest resistance, though none reversed the broader trajectory.

Short Synopsis:
An unauthorized war against Iran expanded while Congress twice failed to reassert its constitutional authority. Election administration tightened under integrity claims, DHS cycled leadership without changing practice, and courts offered the week's most consistent, if partial, resistance.
