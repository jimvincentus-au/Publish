Synopses
--------
Long Synopsis:
Week 83 began when Attorney General Todd Blanche declined to promise Justice Department independence from the White House, a refusal quickly borne out in retaliatory-pattern prosecutions against Kilmar Ábrego García and James Comey. Immigration courts were restaffed with hundreds of "deportation judges," producing mass in-absentia removals. A Trump-appointed regulator approved a bank charter for the president's family crypto venture, while Treasury destroyed a congressionally mandated anti-money-laundering registry. Press freedom absorbed sustained pressure: Pentagon firings at Stars and Stripes, an FCC licensing suit from ABC and Disney, and White House attacks on a CNN reporter's family. Federal courts pushed back on funding overreach, conditioned grants, and mass data transfers. The Democracy Clock moved six seconds, from 7:40 p.m. to 7:40 p.m., as consolidation and constraint roughly canceled out.
