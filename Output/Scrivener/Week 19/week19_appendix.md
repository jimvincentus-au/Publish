# Week 19 Appendix: Citizenship as Leverage

This week shows an aggressive consolidation of executive power, a coordinated campaign against independent institutions, and intensifying stratification of rights by status and identity. Trump’s record pace of executive orders, floated suspension of habeas corpus, and defiance or end-runs around court orders push hard on unchecked presidency and normalized emergency powers. Immigration becomes the central arena: mass-arrest quotas, the planned Office of Remigration, Supreme Court green lights to end parole/TPS, and social-media vetting and visa halts for students collectively move the system toward a tiered citizenship regime. Simultaneously, the administration weaponizes state power against universities, public media, and specific firms, tying funding and access to loyalty while closing analytic and diplomatic capacity inside the State Department. The pardon spree for corrupt officials and wealthy allies, Boeing’s non-prosecution deal, and Trump-linked financial schemes deepen crony capitalism and impunity for elite crime. Yet courts, universities, state AGs, parents, and protest movements mount significant resistance, winning injunctions on tariffs, Harvard, WilmerHale, and media funding. Structurally, however, the week’s net pressure tilts toward entrenching authoritarian tools even as some guardrails still hold.
