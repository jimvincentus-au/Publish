Synopses
--------
Long Synopsis:
Week 74 fused presidential spectacle with serious institutional strain. Trump marked his eightieth birthday with a UFC event on the White House South Lawn, funded partly by federal agencies and tied to his own financial stake, while a triumphal arch rose without congressional approval and renovation costs quietly tripled. Beneath the pageantry, officials weighed suspending habeas corpus and invoking the Insurrection Act after ICE killed two civilians in Minnesota, whose deaths drew no charges even as protesters faced federal conspiracy counts. A loyalist with no intelligence background was installed atop national intelligence after Trump cancelled his own nominee's hearing. Yet courts enforced prior orders, a bipartisan cluster of senators broke publicly with the president over Iran, and Georgia legislators declined an available redistricting advantage. The measured clock held exactly still, reflecting genuine balance rather than calm.

Short Synopsis:
A birthday UFC spectacle, weighed emergency powers, and an intelligence agency handed to a loyalist were met by courts enforcing prior orders and senators breaking with the president. The week's erosive and resistant forces canceled out, leaving the Democracy Clock frozen at 7:41 p.m.
