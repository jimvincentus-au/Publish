This week was defined by a major judicial shock and the rapid political uses that followed. The Supreme Court’s ruling in Louisiana v. Callais weakened a core Voting Rights Act protection, and within days officials in Florida, Louisiana, Alabama, Mississippi, and Tennessee moved to redraw maps, suspend primaries, or call new sessions. At the same time, immigration policy hardened on several fronts: new speech-based green card guidance, continued family detention, a large new funding push for ICE and Border Patrol, and fresh court fights over Temporary Protected Status.
