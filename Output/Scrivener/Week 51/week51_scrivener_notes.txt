Title

One Week, Two Directions, No Motion

Subtitle
--------
The executive reached further than ever into war powers and enforcement while courts, senators, and Minnesota pushed back just enough to hold the clock still.

Tagline / Hook
-------------
The hands did not move, but almost everything did, a week when force expanded and accountability arrived only after the fact.

Synopses
--------
Long Synopsis:
Week 51 held the Democracy Clock at 7:43 p.m., unchanged from the prior week, but that stillness concealed twenty-one separate trait movements. President Trump ordered the unauthorized military capture of Nicolás Maduro, then moved to control Venezuelan oil revenue while a Trump-aligned investor profited from the aftermath. Domestically, a two-thousand-agent immigration surge in Minnesota ended in the fatal shooting of Renee Nicole Good, followed by federal officials branding her a terrorist before any investigation concluded, and the FBI excluding state investigators from the case. The Justice Department fired its own ethics counsel and kept missing Epstein-file deadlines. Yet courts disqualified unlawful appointments, the Senate advanced a War Powers resolution, and Minnesota officials refused to accept the federal account. The offsetting produced no net clock movement, but nearly everything happened.

Short Synopsis:
The Clock held at 7:43 p.m. as unprecedented executive reach, in Venezuela and Minneapolis, met real but incomplete resistance from courts, the Senate, and local officials. Twenty-one trait movements canceled into stillness, not calm.
