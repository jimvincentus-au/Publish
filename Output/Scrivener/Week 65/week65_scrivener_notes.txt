Title
-----
The Clock Holds, Barely

Subtitle
--------
A week of unchecked war powers, whistleblower retaliation, and religious spectacle ends where it began, held level only by scattered resistance.

Tagline / Hook
-------------
The republic didn't fall this week. It just kept absorbing blows, one narrow vote and one lonely judge at a time.

Synopses
--------
Long Synopsis:
Week 65 opened with a presidential blockade of the Strait of Hormuz, ordered without congressional authorization, and closed with an unverified declaration of victory contradicted by Iranian officials. In between, the administration fired judges who ruled against it, issued criminal referrals against a whistleblower and the inspector general who processed his complaint, and moved to erase January 6 seditious conspiracy convictions. Congress narrowly failed to check the war powers, 214-213, but passed bipartisan Haitian protections and forced two ethics resignations. Courts disbarred a fake-elector architect and halted a White House construction project. Inflation and gas prices rose on war-linked costs. Trump posted, then deleted, AI images of himself as Jesus, while attacking the Pope. The clock moved two-tenths of a minute backward, holding nearly level under sustained pressure.

Short Synopsis:
An unauthorized blockade, a false victory claim, retaliation against a whistleblower and inspector general, and religious self-glorification defined the week, while narrow congressional votes, a disbarment, and a construction injunction held the clock nearly level.
