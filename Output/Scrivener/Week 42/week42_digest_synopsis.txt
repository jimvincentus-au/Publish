This was the week the longest government shutdown in American history collided with an election that went badly for the president's party, and the fallout touched nearly every branch of government. SNAP benefits lapsed for 42 million Americans for the first time in 61 years, and the administration spent days resisting, appealing, and ultimately defying court orders to restore them, with the vice president publicly calling a federal judge's ruling 'absurd.' Voters delivered sweeping Democratic wins on Election Day, prompting the president to falsely claim rigged elections, attack Jewish voters, and demand Senate Republicans kill the filibuster to pass voter restrictions.
