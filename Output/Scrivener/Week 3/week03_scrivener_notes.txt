Title
-----
The Week the Government Doubled In Speed

Subtitle
--------
A private operative gained access to Treasury systems as courts, unions, and crowds scrambled to match an administration moving faster than oversight allows.

Tagline / Hook
-------------
When expansion and resistance move at the same furious pace, the clock holds still—but stillness is not the same as calm.

Synopses
--------
Long Synopsis:
In the third week of the second Trump term, executive action arrived in a flood rather than a trickle. Elon Musk's DOGE team, with no confirmation or statutory mandate, gained access to Treasury payment systems, OPM databases, and USAID operations before a federal judge restricted that access. An attempted freeze of trillions in appropriated funds was blocked by another judge and rescinded. Eighteen inspectors general, NLRB and EEOC members, and dozens of DOJ prosecutors were removed in a coordinated purge. An executive order attacking birthright citizenship was blocked by three separate courts. Immigration enforcement expanded past its legal safeguards, press access narrowed toward friendlier outlets, and public records on health and history disappeared. Lawsuits, protests, and judicial rulings pushed back hard enough that the Democracy Clock did not move at all this week, reflecting a term still finding its balance.

Short Synopsis:
A private citizen's team seized access to Treasury payment systems while the administration tried to freeze federal spending, purge inspectors general, and end birthright citizenship. Courts, unions, and protesters pushed back hard enough that the week's damage and resistance canceled out, leaving the Democracy Clock unchanged.
