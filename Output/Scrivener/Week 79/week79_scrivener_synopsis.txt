Immigration enforcement continued with almost no internal accountability, and House Republicans folded voting restrictions into must-pass war-funding bills. Jared Kushner's Saudi financial ties shaped a nuclear agreement largely unexamined by major outlets. Courts provided the week's most consistent resistance, blocking several executive actions, while state attorneys general sued over coercive federal funding conditions. The stalemate between erosion and resistance held, but its durability through an undeclared war remains uncertain.
