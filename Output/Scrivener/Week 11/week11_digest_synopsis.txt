This week combined a slow-building campaign against institutions with a sudden, self-inflicted economic shock. Columbia University capitulated to federal funding threats, placing an academic department under outside control, while similar pressure spread to Harvard and to law firms coerced into settlements. Trump repeatedly floated seeking a third term, testing the 22nd Amendment, even as his own attorney general downplayed the idea. Immigration enforcement produced a string of due-process failures, most visibly the wrongful deportation of Kilmar Abrego Garcia, which the government admitted was an error yet refused to fix until a federal judge ordered his return.
