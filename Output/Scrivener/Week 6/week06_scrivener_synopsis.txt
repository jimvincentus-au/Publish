Week 6 brought no single rupture but a widening pattern from earlier weeks. Elon Musk's DOGE team sought access to IRS and Treasury systems and issued an ultimatum to federal workers, prompting agency defiance, mass resignations, and court injunctions. OPM reversed the ultimatum, only for Trump to formally expand DOGE's authority days later. Pentagon, USAID, and EPA workforces faced sweeping cuts carried out with little coordination. Courts blocked DOGE's data access, DEI enforcement, and mass firings, though compliance was inconsistent. Musk called for a judge's impeachment, Kash Patel took over the FBI, and press access tightened as the White House seized control of the press pool. Citizenship itself came up for redefinition, priced and conditioned on ideology. The clock held at 7:32 p.m., its stillness reflecting a contest, not calm.
