The Democracy Clock held at 7:40 p.m., its first flat week in a long stretch, but twenty-one traits moved beneath that stillness, all but one worsening. A whistleblower's disclosure and several court rulings offset an aggressive push into elections and detainees. Nothing broke. Nothing healed. The pressure simply continued, distributed across enough fronts that no single act looked extraordinary.
