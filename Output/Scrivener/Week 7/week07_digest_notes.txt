Long Synopsis:
This week showed an administration moving on many fronts at once, using financial leverage, immigration enforcement, and executive orders to reshape institutions largely outside ordinary legislative process. Universities faced funding freezes and threats tied to protest policy; a green-card holder and several students were detained or deported for pro-Palestinian speech; and a Maryland man was deported in defiance of a court order and left in a foreign prison. DHS gutted its own civil rights watchdogs. The president fired an independent FTC commissioner, targeted law firms that had represented his opponents, and used a joint address to Congress to unveil a curated culture-war guest list
