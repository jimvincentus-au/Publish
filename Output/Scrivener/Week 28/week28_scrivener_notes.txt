Title
-----
A Week of Broad, Shallow Erosion

Subtitle
--------
Across courts, campuses, and immigration, executive power widened while the clock barely moved, revealing damage measured in breadth, not depth.

Tagline / Hook
-------------
When damage spreads thin across many fronts, the clock stays still even as the ground shifts beneath it.

Synopses
--------
Long Synopsis:
Week 28 of Trump's second term brought no single dramatic rupture but accumulation across a dozen fronts: courts, universities, immigration detention, economic data, media licensing, and the unresolved Epstein-Maxwell affair. The Democracy Clock held nearly still, moving six one-hundredths of a minute, yet two dozen underlying traits worsened. Trump signed a rescissions package clawing back congressionally approved funds while his budget chief floated bypassing Congress entirely through a pocket rescission. He fired the Labor Statistics Commissioner after a weak jobs report. The Senate confirmed Emil Bove to a lifetime judgeship despite allegations he urged defiance of courts. Universities absorbed funding freezes and settlements. Immigration enforcement invoked an eighteenth-century wartime statute to deport migrants without hearings. Courts, whistleblowers, and protesters pushed back, but mostly on defense.

Short Synopsis:
Executive power expanded across money, courts, universities, and immigration this week, while the Democracy Clock barely shifted. The stillness masks broad damage: two dozen traits worsened as institutions bent under simultaneous, uncoordinated pressure.
