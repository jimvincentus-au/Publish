Homeland Security redirected fraud investigators to scrutinize voter rolls, launched a nine-state voter-fraud surge timed to run through early voting, and officials refused to rule out ICE agents at polling places—all built on data later shown to be wildly unreliable. Simultaneously, a new USPS ballot-verification system, developed in defiance of a court order and denounced by an internal whistleblower as catastrophically flawed, was pushed toward the Supreme Court after a district judge blocked it.
