Synopses

Long Synopsis:
The week turned on a Supreme Court decision weakening Voting Rights Act protections, which quickly triggered a chain reaction in state politics. Trump urged states to redraw maps, and by midweek Tennessee had rushed through a new congressional map dismantling its only majority-Black district, while other states moved or signaled they would follow. At the same time, federal power was used aggressively across other fronts: the administration issued a new order targeting mail voting, pursued election-worker data in Georgia, escalated immigration enforcement and detention funding, and kept pressing legal and budgetary support for Trump’s White House ballroom project.
