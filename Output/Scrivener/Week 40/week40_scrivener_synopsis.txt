Week 40 opened with nearly seven million people marching in the largest coordinated demonstration of the Trump era and closed with the longest government shutdown in American history, the White House East Wing reduced to rubble, and a president demanding roughly $230 million in personal compensation from the Justice Department he controls. The administration mocked the protests with monarchist AI imagery rather than engaging them. Courts repeatedly found the government's justifications for domestic troop deployments "untethered to the facts," while the executive branch pushed past those rulings toward the Supreme Court. An elected representative was kept from her seat, redistricting fights spread across three states, and ICE enforcement grew more violent and less accountable. Resistance held in courtrooms, statehouses, and city streets, but did not reverse the trend. The Democracy Clock moved six-tenths of one minute, from 7:41 to 7:42 p.m.
