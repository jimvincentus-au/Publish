Synopses

Long Synopsis:
Week 66 spread executive pressure across new terrain rather than concentrating it in one act. Trump issued and abandoned Iran ceasefire deadlines, rejected War Powers Act constraints, and fabricated a Hormuz reopening claim while posting AI-generated images. The Justice Department installed a loyalist to investigate the president's investigators, indicted the Southern Poverty Law Center, and assigned prosecutors to denaturalize 384 citizens. Hegseth purged senior military leadership. Kushner led Iran diplomacy while receiving over $110 million from Saudi Arabia; Eric Trump promoted a Pentagon contract awarded to his firm. Countervailing action was real but narrow: courts rejected voter-roll demands and a healthcare ban, and Congress passed the Epstein Files Transparency Act. The Democracy Clock moved roughly one minute forward, to 7:42 p.m., a marginal net gain against a week whose dominant current ran the other way.

War conducted by improvisation, prosecutorial retaliation, and a historic denaturalization campaign spread executive pressure into new domains this week. Genuine judicial and legislative checks held at the margins, nudging the Democracy Clock a fraction forward even as the underlying pattern of loyalty over law kept widening its reach.
