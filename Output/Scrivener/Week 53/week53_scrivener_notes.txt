Minnesota Becomes the Proving Ground

Subtitle

Synopses
--------
Long Synopsis:
Week 53's clock barely moved, closing at 7:43 p.m. just as it opened, but that stillness concealed real motion. Minnesota absorbed the largest immigration enforcement operation in U.S. history, producing warrantless home searches, child detentions, and a fatal shooting that triggered statewide protest and an economic blackout. Federal authorities responded to resistance with subpoenas, criminal investigations of Walz and Frey, and charges against church protesters, while a magistrate blocked one overreach against journalist Don Lemon. Beneath these clashes, seventeen inspectors general were fired, an ICE memo claimed warrantless home-entry power, and the White House circulated an altered protester image. Markets, not Congress, forced the week's one clear presidential retreat, on Greenland. The pattern deepened without producing a single decisive break.

Short Synopsis:
Minnesota absorbed the largest immigration surge in U.S. history this week, met by mass protest and an economic blackout. Federal retaliation followed through subpoenas, investigations, and prosecutions, while inspectors general were purged and propaganda tools expanded. The clock held nearly still, masking substantial underlying movement.
