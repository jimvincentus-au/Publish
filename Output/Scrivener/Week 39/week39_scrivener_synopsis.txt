Week 39 unfolded amid a still-active government shutdown, with the administration testing executive power on many fronts at once. National Guard troops moved into Chicago, Portland, Memphis, and Washington despite court orders finding the justifications not credible; the administration appealed and threatened the Insurrection Act. Mass federal layoffs, diverted Pentagon funds, and withheld appropriations turned the shutdown into a political weapon. Congress barely functioned, with the House meeting only twelve of eighty prior days and a duly elected representative left unseated. Officials branded the planned No Kings protest as terrorism, reopened a prosecution against a prominent critic, and imposed press rules that drove nearly the entire Pentagon press corps into a walkout. Courts, a resigning admiral, and several universities pushed back. The Democracy Clock moved six seconds, reflecting a standoff rather than resolution.
