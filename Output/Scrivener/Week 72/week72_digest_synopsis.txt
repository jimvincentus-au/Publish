The week opened with courts checking executive ambition — blocking Alabama's discriminatory map, halting the Kennedy Center takeover, and reopening scrutiny of Trump's IRS settlement — but by midweek the Supreme Court reversed course and let Alabama eliminate a majority-Black district anyway. The most sustained story was Delaney Hall, a New Jersey ICE facility where a detainee hunger strike met escalating force from ICE agents and then state police, pepper-spraying a sitting senator and journalists alike. Congress, in a rare bipartisan moment, forced the administration to abandon its $1.8 billion 'anti-weaponization' fund, though the retreat was incomplete and contested through the week.
