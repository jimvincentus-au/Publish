Mass firings and hiring freezes hollowed out agencies from intelligence and labor to the EPA, NIH, USAID, and the Consumer Product Safety Commission, while new executive orders made it easier to dismiss probationary workers. In parallel, public and international media were sharply constrained: Voice of America was effectively shut down and steered toward a hard‑right contractor, NPR and PBS lost federal funding, and the FCC opened an unprecedented “news distortion” probe into CBS.
