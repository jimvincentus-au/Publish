Long Synopsis:
This week opened with the largest single-day protest in American history and closed with the president's attorney general fired days before a subpoenaed deposition. In between, an unprecedented DHS shutdown reached its 44th day as House Republicans blocked their own Senate's bipartisan funding deal, TSA workers went unpaid for weeks, and Trump signed a series of executive orders and memoranda to route around Congress entirely. Courts pushed back repeatedly — blocking the ballroom project, restoring NPR/PBS funding, restoring detention-facility legal access — even as Trump attacked the Supreme Court ahead of birthright citizenship arguments and attended them in person, an unprecedented act.
