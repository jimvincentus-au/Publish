The week opened with six Democratic veteran-lawmakers reminding troops they may refuse unlawful orders. Trump called this seditious, urged their arrest and execution, and posted messages calling for them to be hanged. Within days, the Pentagon opened a court-martial threat against Senator Kelly and the FBI launched a counterterrorism inquiry into all six. Courts pushed back elsewhere: a federal judge dismissed the Comey and James indictments over an unlawfully appointed prosecutor, and two states saw gerrymandered maps blocked. But immigration enforcement expanded through collective punishment of entire nationalities, a leaked Ukraine peace plan proved substantially Russian-authored, and the White House launched an official tool for naming disfavored journalists. The Democracy Clock closed nearly unchanged, a near-stillness produced by force meeting resistance rather than calm.
