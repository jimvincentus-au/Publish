This week saw the government shutdown harden into a broader struggle over power and accountability. The White House escalated pressure by announcing mass federal layoffs, freezing or canceling billions in funds—disproportionately in Democratic areas—and even canceling major infrastructure and disaster‑protection projects. At the same time, immigration enforcement intensified through military‑style raids, expanded powers for USCIS, and controversial detentions, prompting a series of court orders curbing warrantless arrests and forcing agents to wear body cameras.
