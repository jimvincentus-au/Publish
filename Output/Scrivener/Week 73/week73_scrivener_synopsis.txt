Week 73 moved the clock only six-tenths of a minute, but that stillness concealed sustained strain. Trump ordered and then cancelled Iran strikes within hours, while a general secretly briefed him on invading to seize enriched uranium, all without congressional authorization. He nominated his own former lawyer as Attorney General and tried installing an unqualified ally as intelligence director, a move three Republican senators helped block. Public money flowed toward a White House ballroom, a UFC event, and a revived fund that could compensate January 6 assailants. Courts pushed back repeatedly: an unlawful visa fee fell, the Kennedy Center order held, and a discretionary fund stayed frozen. Kushner and Trump Jr. saw fortunes grow through foreign deals. FISA lapsed amid the fight over intelligence leadership, immigration enforcement expanded with reduced transparency, and a jury acquitted a comptroller for inspecting an ICE facility.
