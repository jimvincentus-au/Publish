This week centered on an intensifying standoff between the executive branch and the courts, universities, and civil society. The Supreme Court repeatedly intervened to halt deportations under the Alien Enemies Act, even as the administration argued it had no duty to correct a wrongful deportation and insisted due process was not required before removal. That defiance culminated Friday in the extraordinary arrest of a sitting Wisconsin judge by the FBI. Harvard became the flagship of institutional resistance, refusing federal demands and suing over a $2.2 billion funding freeze, while over 150 university presidents signed a joint statement against government overreach.
