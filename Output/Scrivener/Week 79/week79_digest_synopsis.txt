Trump publicly floated a 'massive attack' on Iran and threatened to bomb civilian infrastructure, while the House passed war-powers resolutions the Senate declined to make binding. Meanwhile, immigration enforcement continued generating fatal incidents, reversed safety protocols under presidential pressure, and drew fresh lawsuits over surveillance and funding coercion.
