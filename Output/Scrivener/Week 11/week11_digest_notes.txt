Columbia University capitulated to federal funding threats, placing an academic department under outside control, while similar pressure spread to Harvard and to law firms coerced into settlements. Immigration enforcement produced a string of due-process failures, most visibly the wrongful deportation of Kilmar Abrego Garcia, which the government admitted was an error yet refused to fix until a federal judge ordered his return.
