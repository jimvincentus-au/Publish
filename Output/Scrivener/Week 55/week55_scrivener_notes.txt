Title
-----
Stillness That Masks Real Motion

Subtitle
--------
A week of federal enforcement, judicial resistance, and equal pressure on both sides leaves the Democracy Clock exactly where it stood.

Tagline / Hook
-------------
When force and resistance cancel exactly, the clock stops moving, but the ground beneath it does not.

Synopses
--------
Long Synopsis:
Week 55 shows no net movement on the Democracy Clock, holding at 7:43 p.m., but the stillness conceals real activity beneath it. A federal immigration operation in Minneapolis, Metro Surge, continued despite judicial challenge, sweeping up journalists, minors, and bystanders alongside its intended targets. Chicago's mayor ordered oversight of federal agents; Homeland Security promised body cameras. Courts pushed back elsewhere, blocking Haitian TPS termination, restricting force against Portland protesters, and freeing a detained child and his father. Meanwhile, election administration merged with immigration enforcement through voter-roll lawsuits, nationalization rhetoric, and ICE-at-polls suggestions. The administration tightened control over civil servants, pressured Harvard financially, edited a slavery exhibit from a national park, and blurred family business with public office through a crypto sale and tax dispute. Congress applied partial, reactive constraints. Neither side broke through.

Short Synopsis:
Week 55 held the Democracy Clock steady as aggressive immigration enforcement, election-administration coercion, and civil-service politicization pushed forward while courts, Congress, and public protest pulled back with equal force, producing confirmation rather than escalation.
