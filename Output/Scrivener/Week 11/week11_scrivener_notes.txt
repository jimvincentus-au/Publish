Title
-----
Stillness Masking Structural Seizure

Subtitle
--------
A near-flat clock reading concealed the week's true weight, as executive overreach on trade and tenure met real but reactive resistance.

Tagline / Hook
-------------
When five crises open at once, even a democracy's clock can barely register the tremor beneath its feet.

Synopses
--------
Long Synopsis:
The Democracy Clock barely moved this week, closing at 7:33 p.m. after opening at the same time, but the stillness disguised extraordinary volatility. President Trump declared a manufactured national emergency to seize tariff authority from Congress, triggering a $6.6 trillion two-day market collapse. He repeatedly floated a third term, backed by a formal House resolution. The administration admitted wrongfully deporting a legal resident, then defied a court order to return him while attacking the judge. Universities and law firms capitulated to coercion, agencies purged staff, and press outlets faced coordinated pressure. Yet a bipartisan Senate voted against the tariffs, courts blocked several overreaches, and Wisconsin voters rejected Elon Musk's attempt to buy a judicial seat despite unprecedented spending. The near-zero net movement reflects genuine tension between severe erosion and real resistance, not an absence of danger.

Short Synopsis:
A manufactured trade emergency, explicit third-term rhetoric, and an admitted wrongful deportation collided with bipartisan Senate pushback, judicial resistance, and a billionaire's failed vote-buying scheme, leaving the clock nearly frozen despite the week's historic stakes.
