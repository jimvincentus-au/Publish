This week centered on an escalating standoff between the executive branch and the courts over the domestic use of the military. After Trump ordered National Guard troops into Portland and Chicago, federal judges — including Trump's own appointee Karin Immergut — repeatedly blocked the deployments, and the administration repeatedly tried to route around those orders using out-of-state troops. Simultaneously, the Justice Department was reshaped into an instrument of personal retaliation: career prosecutors who found insufficient evidence against James Comey and Letitia James were fired or sidelined, and both were indicted by a newly installed Trump loyalist.
