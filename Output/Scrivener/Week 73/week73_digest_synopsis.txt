This week was defined by the expansion of immigration enforcement power and the institutions straining around it. Congress moved from approving to enacting a $70 billion immigration package with few new guardrails, while ICE oversight weakened, detention protests spread, and reports described harsher consequences for families and children. At the same time, courts repeatedly checked executive overreach: judges blocked the administration’s proposed “anti-weaponization” fund, struck down the $100,000 H-1B fee, and rejected efforts to sidestep legal limits in other disputes.
