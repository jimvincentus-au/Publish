This week unfolded in the shadow of an expanding, undeclared war with Iran, whose closure of the Strait of Hormuz sent gas prices to their sharpest one-month spike in three decades and rippled through global supply chains. Trump described war termination as a matter of gut feeling, dismissed his own intelligence community's threat assessments, and threatened broadcasters and reporters covering the conflict unfavorably, with FCC backing. A senior counterterrorism official resigned publicly, contradicting the war's stated justification, and was swiftly placed under investigation. Congress largely declined to assert oversight, voting down a war-powers resolution and holding no public hearings
