Domestically, the House passed the SAVE America Act tightening voter documentation rules, the FBI searched Fulton County's election office, and DHS pursued naturalized citizens over past voting. Kristi Noem was fired amid a bruising hearing and inspector-general obstruction findings, then replaced by a loyalist senator. DOJ directed lawyers to serve Trump's interests personally. Florida advanced bills treating dissent as a security threat. Courts, including rulings on tariffs, detention oversight, and congressional access, provided the week's clearest resistance, though none reversed the broader trajectory.
