Across the week, the administration pressed aggressive uses of executive power while courts, state officials, and civil society tried to draw lines. Immigration policy was the sharpest flashpoint: the White House invoked an 18th‑century wartime law to deport Venezuelan and Salvadoran migrants, continued flights to El Salvador after a federal judge ordered them halted, and moved to strip legal status from more than half a million immigrants. Judges responded with restraining orders and demands that officials explain apparent defiance.
