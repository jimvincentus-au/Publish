This week's defining thread was the refusal of institutions meant to check the president to actually do so. The Attorney General publicly declined to pledge Justice Department independence. The FCC chair was shown coordinating with the White House against a network he was supposed to regulate neutrally. The Supreme Court paused its own appeals court's ruling to let a personal presidential construction project continue. Meanwhile, courts elsewhere pushed back — on immigration enforcement at churches, on a federal data grab from state driver's records, on retaliatory prosecution theories — showing the judiciary is not uniformly compliant.
