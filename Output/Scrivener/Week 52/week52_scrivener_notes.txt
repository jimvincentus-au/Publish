The administration escalated with funding cuts, retribution threats, and Insurrection Act warnings. Elsewhere, the Justice Department investigated Federal Reserve Chair Jerome Powell, censured a senator over protected speech, and pursued reporters and whistleblowers. Venezuelan oil proceeds flowed to a Trump donor's firm while a Senate war-powers check failed. Courts and states pushed back piecemeal. The Democracy Clock moved only a tenth of a minute, reflecting confirmation of established patterns rather than new invention.
