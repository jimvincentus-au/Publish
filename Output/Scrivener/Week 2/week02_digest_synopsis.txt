This was the administration's opening week of governing, and it moved on nearly every front at once. Trump pardoned roughly 1,500 January 6 defendants, fired more than a dozen inspectors general without legally required notice, and froze federal grants and loans in a maneuver that courts quickly blocked and the administration then muddied by claiming the freeze remained in effect regardless. Immigration enforcement expanded sharply, with ICE raids, arrest quotas, a plan to detain migrants at Guantánamo Bay, and an attempt to end birthright citizenship by executive order.
