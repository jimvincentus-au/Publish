The week opened with a major expansion of federal immigration enforcement: Trump signed a roughly $70 billion law for ICE, Border Patrol, and DHS without new oversight requirements. From there, the week unfolded as a series of contests over how executive power is used and checked. Courts blocked or narrowed some administration moves involving the Kennedy Center, public history at national parks, and retaliatory immigration detention, while other legal fights exposed pressure on unions, researchers, reproductive health providers, and election administration.
