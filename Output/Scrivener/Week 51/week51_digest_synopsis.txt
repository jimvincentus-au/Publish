The week opened with President Trump’s unauthorized military raid to seize Venezuelan president Nicolás Maduro, immediately blurring the line between law enforcement and war and sidelining Congress on the use of force. As Maduro was flown to New York to face U.S. charges, the administration moved quickly to claim control over Venezuelan oil and court U.S. energy companies, while the Senate advanced a bipartisan War Powers challenge. At home, a massive immigration surge in Minnesota culminated in the fatal shooting of legal observer Renee Nicole Good by an ICE agent, followed by a federal takeover of the investigation, exclusion of state authorities, and a wave of protests and vigils…
