In the first week of March 2025, pressure on American democratic institutions intensified across nearly every front simultaneously. ICE detained green-card holders and students for political speech, including Mahmoud Khalil and Yunseo Chung, while Kilmar Ábrego García was deported in defiance of a standing court order. Trump called for impeaching Judge Boasberg, drawing a rare rebuke from Chief Justice Roberts, as Elon Musk and Representative Eli Crane echoed the attack. Universities including Columbia and Penn lost hundreds of millions in funding over protest policies and quickly capitulated. DHS eliminated three oversight offices, collective bargaining rights vanished for a million federal workers, and Trump created a bitcoin reserve benefiting his own holdings. Courts pushed back on foreign aid freezes and an illegal firing, but these corrections were narrow. The Democracy Clock moved six hundredths of a minute, a near-zero net figure masking dozens of simultaneous currents pulling in opposite directions.
