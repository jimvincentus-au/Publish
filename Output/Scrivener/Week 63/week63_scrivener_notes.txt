Title
-----
The Clock Holds, the Pressure Mounts

Subtitle
--------
A record protest and a purged Justice Department pulled against each other while the executive tightened its grip on elections and the military.

Tagline / Hook
-------------
When institutions bend and citizens still march, the hour holds steady, balanced between capture and defense.

Synopses
--------
Long Synopsis:
Week 63 saw a government stretched thin by an unauthorized war and the longest shutdown in history absorb three converging shocks: the firing of Attorney General Pam Bondi and her replacement by a personal loyalist who publicly endorsed presidential control over prosecutions, an executive order asserting federal authority over voter eligibility and mail ballots, and a purge of military leadership for resisting politicized personnel orders. Immigration enforcement grew more violent, with a ruled homicide and tactical units deployed against civilians. Yet courts blocked a White House construction project and struck down retaliatory media funding cuts, while an estimated eight million people joined the largest protest in American history. The Democracy Clock held nearly still, the product of severe erosion offset almost exactly by real resistance.

Short Synopsis:
A record eight-million-person protest and persistent judicial pushback held the line against a purged Justice Department, a national voter-file order, and a politicized military purge, leaving the clock nearly unmoved but far from calm.
