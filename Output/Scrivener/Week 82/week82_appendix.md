Week 82 Appendix: The Clock That Refused to Move
This week combined a major institutional consolidation with escalating security-state normalization and open self-dealing. The Senate's confirmation of Todd Blanche—Trump's former personal lawyer—as attorney general, followed swiftly by DOJ pressure to revive dropped prosecutions and reports of a $1.776 billion 'anti-weaponization fund,' signals accelerating capture of the Justice Department. Simultaneously, DHS/ICE announced procurement of electric-shock gloves and expanded surveillance operations (Operation Puppet Master) targeting progressive organizations, while the State Department revoked over 175,000 visas, including for political speech. Trump's monetization of the presidency reached new visibility: $2.2 billion in personal revenue, a $100,000/month 'Truth API' pay-for-access scheme (now facing lawsuits), a Qatar plane gift, and an IRS settlement granting personal tax immunity. Courts pushed back on some fronts (ballroom construction, Harvard lawsuit dismissal), and primary elections showed voter rejection of election deniers and Trump-endorsed candidates, offering a partial counterweight.
