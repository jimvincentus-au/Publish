The assassination of conservative activist Charlie Kirk on September 10 cast a long shadow over the week, becoming the organizing logic for a wave of executive actions against political opponents, journalists, and institutions. The administration designated Antifa a terrorist organization, threatened visa revocations and prosecutions for critics, and watched as an FCC chair's threats pushed ABC to pull Jimmy Kimmel off air, only to see it return under public pressure. Meanwhile, courts pushed back: judges dismissed Trump's lawsuit against the New York Times, blocked his removal of a Federal Reserve governor, and protected Venezuelan TPS holders.
