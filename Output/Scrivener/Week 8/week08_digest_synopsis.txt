Across seven days, the administration widened its use of executive power on multiple fronts. Immigration enforcement became a central tool of politics: a Palestinian activist, Mahmoud Khalil, and other noncitizens were detained or targeted under rarely used legal provisions, family detention was restarted, refugee resettlement was suspended, and a border emergency and Alien Enemies Act plans signaled more to come. At home, the federal civil service and education system were aggressively reshaped through mass layoffs, civil-rights office closures, and deep cuts to research, food, and health programs, even as courts began to push back on some purges and funding cancellations.
