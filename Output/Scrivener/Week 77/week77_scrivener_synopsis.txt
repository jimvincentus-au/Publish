In a week measuring zero net movement, opposing forces of nearly equal weight pulled against each other. The president fired every remaining commissioner of the Election Assistance Commission, eliminating the last nonpartisan guardian of election machinery months before a midterm. An ICE agent killed an unarmed Houston bystander, and agents then detained the only witnesses. The Fourth of July became partisan spectacle, with toxic fireworks fallout hidden by denial and deletion. Yet courts enforced the Carroll judgment, defended the Kennedy Center ruling, and struck down Florida's speech-restriction law. A housing bill became law despite presidential obstruction. Financial self-dealing through cryptocurrency continued unpunished. The result was balance, not peace: a system where checks still function, but only enough to offset assertion, not reverse it.
