Across seven days, the administration used executive power to reshape core institutions while testing the limits of law and precedent. Trump issued mass pardons for January 6 offenders, dismantled diversity and civil-rights infrastructure, and moved to end birthright citizenship, even as federal courts temporarily blocked some of the most sweeping changes. Immigration enforcement escalated sharply, from ICE quotas and raids in schools and churches to plans for a 30,000-person migrant facility at Guantánamo Bay and the use of trade pressure to force other countries to accept deportees. Inside government, watchdogs and prosecutors were fired, civil servants were pushed toward resignation…
