The week was shaped by a Supreme Court ruling that weakened a key Voting Rights Act protection and quickly triggered a chain reaction in the states. Tennessee rushed through a new congressional map targeting the Memphis district, while Alabama, Louisiana, Mississippi, and others moved or signaled plans to reopen their own maps. At the federal level, the administration paired election-related pressure with broader coercive tactics: a new executive order aimed at mail voting, subpoenas for election-worker data, and continued efforts to gather voter information from states. Immigration enforcement also intensified, from court-defying arrests and court closures to new mass-deportation rhetoric
