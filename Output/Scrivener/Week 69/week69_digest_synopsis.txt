The week was shaped by two overlapping patterns. First, the Supreme Court's recent weakening of Voting Rights Act protections kept rippling outward: Tennessee moved ahead with a map that split Memphis, Alabama got permission to use a disputed map, Louisiana halted an election already underway, and Georgia and South Carolina signaled more redistricting fights to come. Second, the administration kept pressing institutions to bend toward political goals. That pressure showed up in health regulation, immigration courts, public contracting, congressional budgeting, and presidential rhetoric aimed at judges, the press, and political opponents.
