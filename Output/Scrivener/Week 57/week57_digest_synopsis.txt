The week opened with the aftermath of fatal federal shootings in Minneapolis still reverberating: continued enforcement operations, arrests of protesters and journalists, and mounting legal and political pressure that eventually pushed the administration to scale back its Minnesota crackdown. A partial DHS shutdown, triggered by a congressional standoff over ICE accountability reforms, ran through much of the week without resolution. Courts remained an active battleground — blocking deportations, ordering restoration of a dismantled historical exhibit, and repeatedly finding the administration in violation of prior orders. Media freedom came under direct pressure through new FCC guidance
