This week bridged the end of Trump's first 100 days with an accelerating pattern of executive pressure across courts, universities, immigration enforcement, and the press. The arrest of a sitting Wisconsin judge on obstruction charges set an aggressive tone, followed by a wave of executive orders targeting sanctuary cities, campus diversity programs, and public broadcasters. Deportations of U.S. citizen children and green-card holders drew sustained legal challenges, several of which succeeded: courts blocked DOGE's access to Social Security data, restored funds to Radio Free Europe, and permanently barred use of the Alien Enemies Act against Venezuelan migrants in South Texas.
