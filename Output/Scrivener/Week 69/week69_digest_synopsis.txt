This week's dominant story was the rapid, coordinated dismantling of Black-majority congressional districts across the South following the Supreme Court's Louisiana v. Callais decision, which weakened Section 2 of the Voting Rights Act. Tennessee, Louisiana, Alabama, Mississippi, Georgia, and South Carolina all moved within days of each other to eliminate or dilute minority representation, while Louisiana halted an election already underway.
