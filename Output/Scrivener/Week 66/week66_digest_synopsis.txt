This week was defined by expansion and counterpressure. The administration and its allies widened immigration enforcement through new funding, judge firings, denaturalization plans, and a ruling that weakened DACA protections, while Congress, states, and advocacy groups tried to slow or expose those moves through subpoenas, lawsuits, and public protest. At the same time, pressure on the press intensified around FBI Director Kash Patel, with his defamation suit against The Atlantic joined by revelations that the FBI investigated a New York Times reporter who covered him.
