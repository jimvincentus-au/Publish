The week opened with a direct blow to election administration as Trump removed the remaining members of the federal commission that helps states run elections, while the Justice Department warned state election officials they could face prosecution over voter-roll practices. Those moves fed a broader campaign to force action on the SAVE America Act, with voting restrictions tied to unrelated legislation and later attached to a spending bill. At the same time, immigration enforcement became a central story after ICE agents killed two men who were not the targets of operations, prompting a temporary pause in vehicle stops, public protests, and then a White House reversal.
