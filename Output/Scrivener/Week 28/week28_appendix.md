# Week 28 Appendix: Law as Central Command

This week shows an aggressive consolidation of executive power, with emergency authorities and trade tools stretched into quasi-permanent instruments of unilateral rule. Trump’s tariff blitz—via multiple executive orders and a sweeping de minimis suspension—recasts trade law as a personal lever, while courts simultaneously hear challenges to his IEEPA-based tariff authority. Judicial independence takes a major hit: Emil Bove’s confirmation despite whistleblower allegations, DOJ’s misconduct complaint against Judge Boasberg, and manipulated U.S. attorney appointments all signal a judiciary being packed and disciplined for loyalty. Immigration and detention policy harden into a parallel legal order—Alien Enemies Act deportations, Alligator Alcatraz’s legal black hole, abusive ICE practices, and FEMA-funded detention infrastructure—creating stratified citizenship and normalized due-process carve-outs. Universities and media are squeezed through funding freezes, politicized antisemitism mandates, and FCC conditions on mergers, while Oklahoma’s “MAGA exam” and federal religious-expression rules weaponize education and faith. Massive Medicaid and SNAP cuts, plus targeted attacks on gender-affirming care and DEI, deepen inequality and mark vulnerable groups as expendable. Countervailing forces—federal judges blocking some provisions, state AG lawsuits, congressional subpoenas, and investigative journalism—remain active but increasingly embattled.
