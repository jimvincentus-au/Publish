Synopses
--------
Long Synopsis:
The Democracy Clock held at 7:41 p.m. this week, but stillness masked intense activity moving in opposite directions. Trump ordered troops into Portland, told assembled military commanders that American cities could serve as training grounds against an "enemy from within," and declared armed conflict with drug cartels without congressional authorization. Prosecutors who resisted indicting critics were replaced with loyalists; Letitia James was indicted; investigations opened against Bolton and Brennan. A government shutdown became a weapon, with billions impounded from Democratic-led jurisdictions while federal agencies coordinated partisan messaging in apparent Hatch Act violations. ICE intensified enforcement, producing record detentions and due-process violations. Trump distributed racist deepfakes from the White House briefing room. Yet courts blocked FEMA fund reallocation, rejected ending birthright citizenship, and the Supreme Court deferred rather than granted his bid to remove a Fed governor. Resistance held, narrowly.

Short Synopsis:
Executive power expanded across military, prosecutorial, and fiscal fronts this week, while courts, unions, and states pushed back with real effect. The traits roughly balanced, holding the clock steady even as the republic's underlying condition continued to harden.
