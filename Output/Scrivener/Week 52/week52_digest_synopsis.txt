The week's defining event was the killing of Renee Nicole Good, a Minneapolis woman shot by an ICE agent during a massive federal immigration surge. What followed was less a single crisis than a cascading test of institutions: the FBI shut state investigators out of the case, DOJ leadership declined a civil-rights inquiry, and ten or more federal prosecutors and civil-rights officials resigned in protest. The administration defended the shooting, smeared the victim, and threatened Minnesota with 'reckoning' and possible invocation of the Insurrection Act. Nationwide protests met increased force, including tear gas against demonstrators and a second shooting of a bystander. Minnesota
