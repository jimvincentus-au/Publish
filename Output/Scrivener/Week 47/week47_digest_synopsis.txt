This week showed the administration testing the outer edges of executive power while several institutions pushed back. New restrictions hit Afghan and other immigrants, ICE detentions widened into schools, churches, and courthouses, and a citizenship-for-sale proposal drew criticism as a stark stratification of belonging. Health policy saw the hepatitis B vaccine recommendation loosened and ACA subsidy negotiations collapse in the Senate.
