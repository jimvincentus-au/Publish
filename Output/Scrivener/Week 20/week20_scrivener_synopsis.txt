Week 20 produced no net change in the Democracy Clock, but the stillness masked intense activity. Immigration enforcement widened through raids, a nineteen-country travel ban, and a Harvard student visa suspension, even as courts repeatedly intervened to restore due process for wrongly deported migrants. The White House budget director claimed authority to bypass Congress on spending, while the House passed a bill cutting Medicaid and food aid alongside a decade-long block on state AI regulation. Agencies tied to care and warning, FEMA, the National Weather Service, Veterans Affairs, were hollowed out through staffing cuts. A public feud between President Trump and Elon Musk exposed how much government capacity depends on personal alliance. Courts held some lines, but Congress's spending power came under direct, unresolved challenge.
