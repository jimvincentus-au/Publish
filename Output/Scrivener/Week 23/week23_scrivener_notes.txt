Synopses
--------
Long Synopsis:
Week 23 opened with President Trump ordering strikes on Iranian nuclear facilities without congressional authorization, then dismissing his own intelligence agencies when their assessments proved inconvenient. Congress tabled impeachment articles rather than confront the war-powers question directly. Domestically, ICE intensified raids across Los Angeles and beyond, using masked officers and expanding detention infrastructure in Florida and California. A detainee died in custody; a pregnant woman lost her child after medical requests were ignored. A DOJ whistleblower alleged a senior nominee suggested defying court orders, while the department sued Maryland's entire federal bench. The Supreme Court narrowed nationwide injunctions, Voice of America lost hundreds of staff, and vaccine advisory panels were remade with skeptics. Courts still ruled against the administration in specific cases, but the week's dominant current ran one direction: toward greater executive latitude and thinner accountability.

Short Synopsis:
A unilateral strike on Iran, expanding immigration raids, and a purge of vaccine advisers marked a week of consolidation rather than new direction. Courts pushed back in places, but oversight narrowed across war powers, information control, and institutional independence alike.
