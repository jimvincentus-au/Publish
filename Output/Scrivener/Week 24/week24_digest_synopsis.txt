This week centered on the passage of the sprawling budget reconciliation bill—the 'One Big Beautiful Bill Act'—which moved from Senate debate to final passage and presidential signature by July 4. Along the way it absorbed nearly every other storyline: deep Medicaid and SNAP cuts, a massive expansion of ICE funding, tax cuts weighted toward wealthy Americans, and procedural maneuvers that bypassed the Senate parliamentarian. Simultaneously, the Supreme Court limited nationwide injunctions in a birthright citizenship case, opening room for executive immigration actions to proceed unevenly across states, and separately cleared deportations to South Sudan.
