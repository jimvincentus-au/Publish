The week moved on several tracks at once, but they reinforced one another. The administration sought more money and political room for the Iran war even as questions grew about conflicts of interest, market bets, and the costs of the conflict at home. Immigration policy hardened further through funding plans, judge firings, federal deployments, denaturalization plans, and a new ruling that could make DACA recipients easier to deport. At the same time, pressure on accountability institutions widened: Congress pursued ethics and conflict inquiries, watchdogs and states filed new transparency and records suits, and courts both checked and enabled executive action.
