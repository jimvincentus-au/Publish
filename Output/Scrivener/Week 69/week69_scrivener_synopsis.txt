Week 69 began with a Supreme Court reversal and ended with an entire minority caucus stripped of its committee seats. Between those points, a weakened voting-rights standard became, within days, concrete losses for Black representation across Louisiana, Tennessee, Georgia, Mississippi, Alabama, and South Carolina. Louisiana went further, suspending an active primary after ballots were already cast. Elsewhere, the administration continued war operations past a statutory deadline, considered renaming the conflict to evade it, and presented Congress with war-cost figures that shifted mid-testimony. Public money flowed to a golf-club-connected contractor and an undisclosed UFC fundraiser. Real checks held too: a mifepristone ruling survived, a state senate blocked a targeted map, and a party censured its own governor. The clock closed exactly where it opened, concealing a week of real, unevenly distributed damage.
