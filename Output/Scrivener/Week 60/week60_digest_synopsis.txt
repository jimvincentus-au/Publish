This week was dominated by a war the president started without asking Congress and could not consistently explain. Trump launched military operations against Iran, then delivered contradictory public statements about surrender, war aims, and duration, while officials misstated Tomahawk missile facts, casualty counts, and Navy operations in the Strait of Hormuz. Oil and gas prices spiked sharply, costing consumers billions as war expenses climbed past $11 billion in six days. Simultaneously, Trump pressured Congress to pass the Save America Act, a sweeping voting-restriction bill, threatening to block all other legislation and pushing the Senate to gut the filibuster.
