This was a week in which the Supreme Court did two contradictory things at once. In a single sitting, it stripped away a 90-year-old shield protecting independent agencies from at-will presidential removal, handing Trump sweeping new control over regulators — while also striking down his executive order on birthright citizenship, blocking his removal of a Federal Reserve governor, and protecting mail-in ballots from a partisan challenge. The rulings, taken together, describe a judiciary still capable of drawing some lines but increasingly willing to hand the presidency others.
