Title
-----
The Week the Levers Multiplied

Subtitle
--------
Federal troops in Washington, a gerrymander pushed through by force, and a purge of unwelcome facts moved together across a single week.

Tagline / Hook
-------------
No single act broke the republic this week. The accumulation of ordinary-looking orders did the work instead.

Synopses
--------
Long Synopsis:
Week 31 brought no single rupture, only accumulation across nearly every institution at once. Trump deployed National Guard troops and federal agents to seize control of Washington's police department, while Texas Republicans used surveillance and court orders to force through a Trump-requested gerrymander over Democratic resistance. Elsewhere, the administration pursued mail-voting restrictions tied to Putin's talking points, fired the Bureau of Labor Statistics commissioner over an unfavorable jobs report, revoked security clearances from three dozen intelligence officials, and expanded ideological vetting across immigration and citizenship systems. Courts offered partial resistance: blocking detention-facility expansion, restraining a Ten Commandments mandate, and releasing a wrongly deported man pending trial. The Democracy Clock barely moved, a tenth of a minute, because nothing here was unprecedented. Everything here was confirmed.

Short Synopsis:
Federal troops occupied Washington's streets, Texas forced through a gerrymander by coercion, and truth-telling institutions from labor statistics to intelligence absorbed loyalty purges. Courts pushed back at the margins. The clock barely moved because the week deepened patterns already in motion rather than opening new ones.
