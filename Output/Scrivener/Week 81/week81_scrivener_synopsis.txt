This week the Democracy Clock barely moved, but the underlying record showed continued strain across multiple fronts. Todd Blanche's confirmation as attorney general advanced only after a contested rescission of a fund tied to protecting Trump's family from tax audits. Executive orders sought to narrow birthright citizenship weeks after the Supreme Court affirmed it.
