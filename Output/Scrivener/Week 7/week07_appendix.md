# Week 7 Appendix: Crony Capitalism as Governance

This week shows an aggressive consolidation of executive and crony power, paired with systematic hollowing-out of the civil service, regulatory state, and foreign policy apparatus. Trump and Musk’s DOGE machine drives mass layoffs and attempted dismantling of core agencies (Education, Social Security, VA, USPS, CFPB, IAF), while crypto and tariff policies openly intertwine public power with private enrichment. National security is reoriented away from Russia and Ukraine toward a Russia-friendly posture, with cyber operations halted, intelligence sharing curtailed, and aid to Ukraine paused or impounded. Domestically, immigration, DEI, LGBTQ, and reproductive rights are targeted through executive orders, enforcement practices, and DOJ pressure on universities, stratifying citizenship and weaponizing law against disfavored groups. Courts and some independent bodies push back—blocking foreign-aid freezes, illegal firings, and some mass terminations—showing institutional resistance but also triggering retaliatory impeachment threats and smear campaigns. Trade policy whiplash and a politicized crypto reserve inject economic chaos that offloads risk onto the public. Civil society mobilizes in protests and state-level resistance, but the structural pressure this week is overwhelmingly toward entrenched authoritarian governance and oligarchic capture.
