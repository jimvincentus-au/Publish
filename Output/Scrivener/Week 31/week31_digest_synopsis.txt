This week's defining story was Washington, D.C. itself: federal agents and National Guard troops from six Republican-led states occupied the capital under a crime emergency contradicted by 30-year-low violence statistics, while investigators simultaneously probed whether the DC police manipulated crime data. That contradiction — declaring an emergency while investigating the very numbers used to justify it — ran through much of the week. In Texas, Democratic lawmakers who fled the state to block a Trump-directed redistricting map returned to face police escorts, confinement, and a chamber standoff before Republicans passed the map anyway.
