The week was defined by two overlapping patterns. First, the Supreme Court's recent Callais ruling continued to reverberate through state politics, as Alabama, Tennessee, Louisiana, Georgia, and South Carolina moved quickly to revisit maps, suspend elections, or test how far weakened voting-rights protections would now allow them to go. Second, the administration kept widening executive pressure across institutions: the FDA faced political interference, immigration enforcement expanded through secrecy and offshore detention, Congress fought over a large enforcement package tied to Trump's White House ballroom project, and Trump publicly pressured the courts while escalating rhetoric
