The week was defined by a sharp split between executive escalation and institutional resistance. Federal judges repeatedly blocked Trump administration efforts to centralize control over elections, including proof-of-citizenship rules, federal voter-list schemes, and mail-ballot restrictions. At the same time, the administration widened its use of coercive rhetoric and state power: Trump tied a bipartisan housing bill to voting restrictions, installed a loyalist as acting intelligence chief, and formally cast “antifa” and protest activity in terrorism terms.
