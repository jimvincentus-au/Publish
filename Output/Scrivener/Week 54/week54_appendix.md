Structurally, this week exerts heavy pressure on traits 4, 8–9, 11, 41, 45, 46, 49–54, and 9/11‑style national‑security exceptionalism. Civil society mobilization—from national anti‑ICE strikes to Minnesota’s economic blackout—demonstrates that democratic antibodies remain active even as executive overreach and security‑state impunity deepen.
