Title
-----
Testing How Far Defiance Travels

Subtitle
--------
A wartime deportation law, a judge under siege, and a Chief Justice's rare rebuke defined a week measured almost to a standstill.

Tagline / Hook
-------------
When a plane full of deportees ignored a judge's order, the question was no longer about policy—it was whether courts still bind the executive at all.

Synopses
--------
Long Synopsis:
The week opened with the invocation of the 1798 Alien Enemies Act to deport hundreds of Venezuelan and Salvadoran men without hearings, and closed with Chief Justice Roberts publicly defending a judge under attack. Between those points, deportation flights proceeded despite a judge's restraining order, a Maryland resident with protected status was wrongly sent to a Salvadoran prison, and immigration authority was turned against student activists and academics. The administration dismantled the Department of Education and Voice of America by executive order, coerced a major law firm into concessions, and pressed universities with funding cuts. Federal judges pushed back repeatedly—on USAID, Social Security data access, and a transgender military ban—while Congress mostly amplified executive action rather than checking it. The clock barely moved, reflecting a week where erosion and resistance ran nearly even.

Short Synopsis:
A wartime deportation law, defied court orders, and an unprecedented attack on a federal judge defined the week, even as courts, attorneys general, and a rare rebuke from the Chief Justice held part of the line. The clock moved only two-tenths of a minute.

Actionable Outcomes
-------------------
- Contact senators and representatives to oppose impeachment efforts against judges based on disputed rulings rather than misconduct.
- Support organizations providing legal representation to detained immigrants and international students facing due process violations.
- Follow and support litigation by state attorneys general challenging the dismantling of federal agencies like the Department of Education.
- Contact congressional representatives to demand oversight hearings on Alien Enemies Act deportations and compliance with court orders.
- Support independent journalism outlets affected by the defunding of Voice of America and related broadcasters.
- Document and report any threats or harassment directed at judges or their families to appropriate law enforcement and judicial security offices.
- Engage with civil liberties organizations tracking due process cases involving green card holders and visa holders detained over speech.
