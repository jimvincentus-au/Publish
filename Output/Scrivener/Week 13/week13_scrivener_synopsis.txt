Week 13 brought the sharpest executive-judicial confrontation of the term. The administration publicly signaled it would not comply with a unanimous Supreme Court order to facilitate the return of a wrongly deported man, with officials misstating the ruling in court filings. Courts escalated in response, including a criminal contempt finding and a unanimous conservative-majority appellate rebuke. Trump repeatedly floated deporting American citizens to Salvadoran prisons. Harvard refused federal demands and had billions in funding frozen within hours. A governor's home was firebombed while his family slept; a sitting judge was arrested in handcuffs. Press freedom, the Federal Reserve, and civil service protections all came under sustained pressure. The clock moved only two-tenths of a minute, but the forces pulling in opposite directions were the strongest yet measured.
