Title

The Clock Holds, Barely, Under War

Subtitle
--------
A week of unauthorized war escalation, ballot seizures, and airport dragnets tested a democracy already worn thin, without moving the needle.

Tagline / Hook
-------------
A president confessed to dodging the Constitution's one check on war, while the clock stood still and the pressure did not.

Synopses
--------
Long Synopsis:
Week 62 recorded no net change in the Democracy Clock, holding at 7:43 p.m., but stillness masked accumulation rather than calm. The president threatened Iranian civilian infrastructure under a 48-hour ultimatum, then admitted at a fundraiser he avoided the word "war" specifically to dodge congressional authorization. ICE agents replaced airport security nationwide, ensnaring a mother and her citizen daughter. A California sheriff seized 650,000 ballots on unproven fraud claims. The House passed the SAVE America Act while Trump pushed to eliminate the filibuster to force it through. Courts blocked unlawful detention policy, reinstated a wrongly deported man, and voided an illegal vaccine panel. War costs spiked fuel prices and cut benefits at home even as sanctions eased on adversaries abroad. Resistance held some lines; damage accumulated along familiar grooves, deepened and more openly stated.

Short Synopsis:
An undeclared war escalated as Trump admitted evading constitutional authorization, while ICE moved into airports, a sheriff seized ballots, and Congress fought over voting restrictions. Courts pushed back on several fronts. The Democracy Clock held at 7:43 p.m., unmoved but under mounting strain.
