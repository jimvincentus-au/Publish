This was a week defined by a widening gap between presidential rhetoric and institutional pushback. Trump publicly demanded the arrest and execution of Democratic lawmakers over a video reminding troops they can refuse unlawful orders, and the Pentagon and FBI followed with investigations into Senator Mark Kelly. At the same time, courts pushed back hard: a federal judge threw out the prosecutions of James Comey and Letitia James for unlawful appointment of a loyalist prosecutor, a Georgia prosecutor dropped the last remaining 2020 election case against Trump, and federal panels blocked gerrymandered maps in Texas and Utah.
