Week 80 showed no singular rupture, only accumulation. After the Supreme Court struck down emergency tariffs, the administration invoked two successive statutory authorities to preserve them. The Pentagon reclassified Iran war casualties, appearing to reset the clock triggering congressional review. A cabinet nomination stalled when senators demanded the president dissolve a personal tax-immunity fund. Federal advisors reached into the Smithsonian, and Section 106 protections for historic sites were gutted. Immigration enforcement expanded against protected populations. A fabricated story about Mayor Mamdani spread across major outlets before quiet correction. Yet courts blocked election overreach, a unanimous Senate rejected clemency for a trafficker, and a labor leader's charges were dropped. The Democracy Clock held nearly still, moving one-tenth of a minute, as pressure and friction nearly canceled out.
